# Phase 1 independent same-group term evidence

Date: 2026-09-15

Scope: a numeric random intercept and random slope represented as separate
covariance terms sharing one grouping factor.

## Public contract

The accepted forms

```text
Reaction ~ Days + (1 + Days || Subject)
Reaction ~ Days + (1 | Subject) + (0 + Days | Subject)
```

produce the same compact group-major design and identical ML/REML fits. The
specification records covariance-term sizes `(1, 1)`, yielding two nonnegative
theta parameters and an exactly zero intercept/slope prior covariance. Kamino
does not split the solve: nonzero intercept/slope cross-products remain in each
subject block, preserving the likelihood coupling implied by the shared rows.
Formulae receives only the fixed formula and no grouping column; no dense `Z`
is materialized by the production adapter.

## Pinned lme4 comparisons

The reviewed fixture is `oracle/fixtures/v1/sleepstudy_independent.json`,
generated by the digest-pinned lme4 2.0-6 oracle. Ten fixed-theta cases cover ML
and REML at zero, intercept-only, slope-only, positive diagonal, and near-slope-
boundary factors. The maximum objective difference is `4.55e-13`, within the
declared `1e-9` tolerance, and each case independently agrees with dense marginal
algebra.

Optimized Sleepstudy ML and REML results pass the pinned objective, beta, theta,
residual-scale, covariance, conditional-mode, fitted-value, and prediction
checks. Maximum absolute differences are:

| Quantity | Maximum difference | Acceptance |
|---|---:|---:|
| Objective | `4.72e-9` | `1e-6` |
| Theta | `1.32e-5` | `2e-5` |
| Random covariance element | `0.0157` | `atol=0.02`, `rtol=1e-4` |
| Conditional mode | `3.54e-4` | `1e-3` |
| Conditional prediction | `3.54e-4` | `1e-3` |

Kamino records SciPy Powell as its optimizer and does not imply it ran lme4's
pinned NLopt BOBYQA configuration.

## Boundary, persistence, and resources

A separate Kamino-owned weighted/offset synthetic case has a positive random-
intercept scale and exact zero random-slope scale under both ML and REML. The
maximum objective difference is `7.39e-13`; Kamino returns the second theta and
the corresponding covariance diagonal as exactly zero with boundary diagnostics.

Prediction bundle schema 1.1 introduced retention of `(1, 1)`; current schema
1.2 continues to round-trip independent ML/REML predictions exactly. Legacy
schema 1.0 remains readable as its original single correlated term. The
manifested million-row/10,000-group benchmark now
includes the independent structure under ML and REML; its reviewed measurements
are recorded in `docs/evidence/phase1-single-group-resource.md`.

Categorical double-bar expansion, random terms with different grouping factors,
and more general repeated-term syntax remain fail-closed and unclaimed.
